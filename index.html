How to use W2G AutoFill

The problem it solves: you keep typing the same client details into the same PDF forms. Set up a form once, then every client is one click.

1 · Add your clients (once per client)

  1. Open Clients and drop in an ePartner policy printout (PDF), a customer list (Excel / CSV / PDF table), or a photo / screenshot.
  2. The app shows what it found. OK = read cleanly, Check = found but please confirm (e.g. phone missing its leading 0), Missing = not in the file — it stays blank, the app never guesses.
  3. Press Add to save them. Tap a client any time to correct a detail.

2 · Set up a form (once per form)

  1. Open Forms → New form from blank PDF and choose the empty form. For the Great Eastern Nominee (NBZ-FNOMI) and Withdrawal/Surrender (CSD-PSF33) forms the app offers a ready-made layout.
  2. For any other form: choose What goes here? (e.g. Full name), choose Normal text or One letter per box, then drag a rectangle over the space. For boxes, cover the whole row — the app finds each little box.
  3. Pick a client under Check the layout to see real values on the page, adjust, then Save template.

3 · Fill and download (every time)

  1. Open Fill & download, choose the form and tick one or more clients, press Fill & preview.
  2. Look at the preview. Anything red or amber can be typed in right there (this changes only this form, not your client list).
  3. Tick I have checked this form, then Download PDF (on a phone you can also Share it).
Left blank on purpose: signatures, witness details and tick-box choices (e.g. withdrawal type, reasons) — the client completes those.
Your data: client files are read inside this browser and saved only on this device. Nothing is uploaded. Use Clients → Clear all to remove everything. To move a form layout to your phone, use Export on the template and Import template file on the phone.
